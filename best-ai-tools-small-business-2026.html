Which tool should you choose?

1. Canva — visual marketing

Useful for creating social posts, flyers, presentations and simple brand assets without advanced design experience. Explore its AI-assisted creative features if visual content is your bottleneck.

Best fit: A business owner who regularly needs graphics.

Explore Canva

2. Notion — organizing work

Combines notes, project tracking and documentation. Its AI capabilities can assist with drafting and summarizing, depending on the plan and workspace settings.

Best fit: A small team managing repeatable projects.

Explore Notion

3. Grammarly — business writing

Can help refine emails, proposals and other writing. Always review suggested edits for accuracy, tone and confidential information.

Best fit: Someone writing customer-facing content every day.

Explore Grammarly

4. HubSpot — customer management

A CRM and marketing platform with AI-assisted capabilities. It can be useful once you have leads to track and follow-up processes to organize.

Best fit: A business with a growing customer pipeline.

Explore HubSpot

5. Hostinger — getting a website online

Offers website hosting and website-building options, including AI-assisted creation tools on eligible products. Compare the initial and renewal costs before committing.

Best fit: A beginner who wants to launch a website.

Explore Hostinger

How to pick without wasting money

  • Choose one immediate problem: website, graphics, writing, organization or CRM.
  • Try a free plan or trial when available; verify current terms on the provider's site.
  • Check what is actually included in the plan, especially AI usage limits.
  • Review cancellation terms, data privacy and renewal pricing.
  • Measure whether the tool saves enough time or generates enough value to justify its cost.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need all five tools?

No. Most new businesses should start with the one that solves their biggest current problem.

Are AI tools completely hands-off?

No. AI outputs need review, especially for factual claims, customer communication and sensitive information.

Are these tools free?

Free offerings and trials vary by provider and can change. Check the current plan pages before signing up.
